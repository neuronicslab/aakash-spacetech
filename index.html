AAKASH: SpaceTech and Scientific Discovery

A joint IISc–ARTPARK cluster taking research from the laboratory to flight-ready payloads

About the Cluster

India's space sector has been expanding into a public–private innovation ecosystem since the 2020 reforms. High-value growth will be driven by specialised, mission-focused capabilities in hosted payloads, in-space manufacturing, microgravity science, orbital servicing and lunar surface operations. However, India lacks a systematic engine outside ISRO to translate academic research beyond TRL 4–6 into flight-ready payloads.

IISc and ARTPARK are jointly developing this cluster to bridge these critical gaps. The cluster focuses on payloads and space environments rather than launch vehicles or satellite sales. IISc leads fundamental research up to TRL 3, while ARTPARK handles translation and flight from TRL 4 onwards. All work moves through a single stage-gated programme operating as a hub-and-spoke model in close partnership with ISRO, IN-SPACe and industry.

Vision

To build the foundations of India's Space Economy 2.0 through agile collaboration across academia, government and industry, delivering mission-critical capabilities that will define the next era, researched, built and flown from Indian soil.

Mission

To establish a National Space-Tech Capability Hub that integrates fundamental research, shared engineering platforms, a robust payload pipeline and venture building, spanning TRL 1 through to in-orbit demonstration.

Lunar Surface Operations

Systems and science for operating on the Moon's surface.
